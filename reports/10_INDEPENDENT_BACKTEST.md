## 2. Method

Boards are processed in decision-time order (`generated_at`, else commit time). For every fixture × market on every board the independent rule decides, with integrity gates stricter than production: real finite quote > 1.0, probability in (0,1), decision strictly before kickoff. **The first board that selects a (fixture, market, side) is that bet's single first-sight decision**: price, edge and tier are frozen there and later boards are duplicates (`n_boards_selected` counts them). A "policy book" then keeps one O/U 2.5 side per fixture and one goal direction per fixture (first sight wins), applied to the whole history so no fixture is double counted. Every independent bet is settled flat 1u at its first-sight price: win = odds − 1, loss = −1, void = 0 and excluded from stake, unknown result = unsettled (never 0, never a loss).

Reconciliation key = (match_date, home, away, market, side) — the ledgers' own key. Every production key that ever existed in the ledger history (2,481) is compared to the independent first-sight book, and every independent bet inside each ledger's operating window that production does not have is listed (11).

Reading:
* **Standard O/U (legacy model)** does worse than betting random sides of the same fixtures, and its exact opposite side loses less (−4.86% vs −10.38%). No selection skill is demonstrated; the CI includes zero only narrowly on the high side.
* **NF O/U** is about 4pp better than random-side / random-fixture placebos, but p ≈ 0.17–0.20: not distinguishable from chance.
* **NF BTTS** beats random equal-size BTTS-YES subsets of the same window (p = 0.010, one comparison of five, so treat as p ≈ 0.05 after a Bonferroni-style allowance) while its own day-block CI since 09-01 includes zero ([−2.6, +22.8]). That is PROMISING-UNCONFIRMED, not confirmed; it needs the pre-registered untouched forward period (phase 9).
* **O1.5 NF** is indistinguishable from the O1.5 market base rate in that window (random priced fixtures also earn +8.35%): the profit belongs to the market/regime, not to the selection.
* The outcome-permutation control keeps each book's hit rate and shuffles which odds win; it tests the price↔outcome pairing. No book is distinguishable from it.
