## Smarter Formulas

- **Dixon-Coles ρ correction for the 2.5 boundary**: The over/under probabilities computed in `betting.evaluate_value()` and the formulas in `agent/formulas.md §1` use independent Poisson (λ_H, λ_A). Dixon-Coles showed that joint probability at low goal counts is systematically mis-estimated by independent Poisson — specifically, (0,0), (1,0), (0,1), (1,1) are more probable than Poisson predicts. The correction is: `τ(x, y, λ_H, λ_A, ρ) = 1 - λ_H×λ_A×ρ` for (0,0); `1 + λ_H×ρ` for (1,0); `1 + λ_A×ρ` for (0,1); `1 - ρ` for (1,1); `1` for all others. With ρ ≈ −0.08, P(total ≤ 2) is reduced slightly, increasing P(over 2.5). Add `src/poisson.py:dixon_coles_p_over25(lambda_h, lambda_a, rho=-0.08)` and use it in the formula library. Why: the current model's Poisson calibration is worst in the 2–3 goal zone, exactly where our VALUABLE/MARKSMAN signals live. The DC correction is mathematically sound and costs nothing.

- **Bookmaker overround as a model feature**: In `src/feature_engineering.py:build_features()` (line 430–432), we already compute `implied_prob_over = 1/odds_over25` and `implied_prob_under = 1/odds_under25`. Their sum minus 1 is the bookmaker's overround: `overround = (1/odds_over + 1/odds_under) - 1`. A high overround (>10%) signals a match the bookmaker is uncertain about — these are the markets with the most mispricing opportunity. A low overround (<4%) signals a Pinnacle-style sharp market that's already well-priced. Add `feat["bookmaker_overround"] = feat["implied_prob_over"] + feat["implied_prob_under"] - 1` in both `build_features()` and `build_upcoming_features()`, add it to `FEATURE_COLS` in `src/model.py`. Why: current features see the prices but not the margin — overround is orthogonal information the GBM isn't seeing.
